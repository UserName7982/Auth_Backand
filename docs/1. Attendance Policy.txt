1. Attendance Policy
Document: attendance_policy.md
Academic Policy
Department of Academic Affairs
Student Attendance Policy
1. Every student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject to be eligible for semester-end examinations.
2. Attendance is calculated separately for every subject, including theory and practical classes.
3. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation, subject to institutional rules and approval.
4. Students with attendance below 65% are generally not eligible for semester-end examinations unless an officially approved exception applies.
5. Medical leave must be supported by valid medical documents submitted to the department.
6. Attendance records are maintained by the respective subject faculty members.
7. Students should report attendance discrepancies to their faculty within seven working days of the recorded absence.

2. Examination Policy
Document: examination_policy.md
Examination
Examination Rules and Regulations
1. Students must carry a valid college identity card and examination admit card.
2. Students should reach the examination centre at least 30 minutes before the examination begins.
3. Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators (unless permitted), and unauthorized electronic devices are prohibited.
4. Students are not allowed to leave the examination hall during the first 30 minutes.
5. Any student found engaging in unfair means may face disciplinary action according to university regulations.
6. Internal assessment may include assignments, quizzes, attendance, presentations, and class tests.
7. Students must complete all required internal assessments to remain eligible for the final examination.

3. Fee Payment Policy
Document: fee_policy.md
Finance
College Fee Payment Guidelines
1. Semester fees must be paid before the deadline announced by the administration.
2. Students can pay fees through the official online payment portal or approved bank payment methods.
3. Students must retain their payment receipt for future reference.
4. Late fee charges may apply to payments made after the prescribed deadline.
5. Students facing financial difficulties may contact the accounts department regarding available assistance.
6. Fee refund requests must be submitted through the prescribed application process.
7. Refund eligibility and amounts are governed by the applicable university and institutional regulations.

4. Library Policy
Document: library_policy.md
Library
College Library Rules
1. Every enrolled student can access the college library using a valid identity card.
2. Students can borrow up to three books simultaneously.
3. The standard borrowing period is 14 days.
4. Books must be returned or renewed before the due date.
5. Late returns may result in fines according to library regulations.
6. Reference books, journals, and rare collections cannot be taken outside the library.
7. Students are responsible for damaged or lost books issued under their accounts.
8. Silence must be maintained inside the library.

5. Leave Application Policy
Document: leave_policy.md
Administration
Student Leave Application Guidelines
1. Students requiring leave must inform their respective class coordinators.
2. Leave applications should mention the reason, duration, and supporting documents where applicable.
3. Medical leave exceeding three consecutive days should be supported by a medical certificate.
4. Leave approval does not automatically guarantee attendance relaxation.
5. Students are responsible for completing assignments and academic work missed during approved leave.
6. Applications should be submitted through the designated college procedure.
